How to Earn Your Free License

After the initial beta period ends, the program will transition to an Early Access model. To maintain free access, testers must periodically provide feedback on new functionalities, report bugs, and suggest enhancements. Active participation is required—simply having the software is not enough. We value testers who continuously help us improve LoOper through ongoing, meaningful engagement with each update.

*Free lifetime licenses are awarded to beta testers who demonstrate consistent, quality participation throughout the beta period.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in all plans?

Every plan includes all LoOper features: visual workflow editor, recording, playback, AI integration, scheduling, sandboxing, and unlimited workflows.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade from Subscription to Individual or Corporate at any time. Contact us for migration options.

How does the volume discount work?

Corporate licenses get progressive discounts: 5% for 10+ devices, 10% for 25+, 15% for 50+, 20% for 100+, and 25% for 250+ devices.

Is there a free trial?

Apply for our Beta Program to get free access during the testing phase, or contact sales for a corporate evaluation license.
